The path to a resolution.

How every committee session unfolds, from the first roll call to the final vote.

  1. 01

    Roll Call

    The chair confirms which delegations are present and voting.

  2. 02

    Opening Speeches

    Each delegate sets out their country’s position on the topic.

  3. 03

    Caucus

    Moderated and unmoderated caucuses to debate, negotiate and form blocs.

  4. 04

    Working Papers

    Blocs turn their ideas into a draft resolution with sponsors and signatories.

  5. 05

    Amendments

    Delegates refine the text clause by clause, friendly or unfriendly.

  6. 06

    Voting

    The committee votes. With a majority, the resolution passes.
